# Product

## Platform

web

## Register

Experience. The portfolio work leads from the first viewport; interface recedes.

## Product

Wreative Showcase: portfolio gallery of websites, web apps and mobile apps built by Wreative (Indonesia). Browse, search, filter by category, open a project page with image/video/Lottie gallery and tags.

## Users

- Prospective clients (business owners, mostly Indonesian) deciding whether to hire Wreative.
- Employers and collaborators judging craft and technical skill.
Both weighted equally.

## Tone

Minimal, clear, professional. Specific language, no marketing buzzwords.

## Constraints

- Bilingual: English and Indonesian (src/i18n/ui.ts; project descriptions have `description` + `descriptionId`).
- Light and dark theme.
- Brand palette: slate blue, light neutral, restrained coral accent (see design.md).
- Static Astro + Tailwind site, PWA; no backend.
- Content lives in src/data/portfolio/*.ts; do not invent projects, stats or testimonials.
